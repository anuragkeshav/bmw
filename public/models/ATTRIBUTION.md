The redistributor states that the original model was deduplicated, pruned, welded, and Meshopt-compressed with glTF Transform, and its textures resized to 2K (19.7 MB → 3.5 MB). This website downloads that optimized GLB unchanged. At runtime, the viewer recenters and scales the model, omits its unused helper object, clips a known disconnected piece of splitter geometry above the roof, and adjusts paint, glazing, tire, carbon, metallic, and lighting materials for the editorial presentation. Color controls modify only `M4xNME_Paint`; the original CSL red detailing, badges, wheels, and glazing remain separate.

The cinematic update also reparents the original `M4xNME_hood_csl` to an animated hinge, revealing the asset's original engine, intake, strut brace and engine-bay geometry. Engine components receive graphite, matte-black and brushed-aluminium presentation materials. Procedurally generated headlamp halos, exhaust smoke and additional studio lights are original website effects, not changes to the GLB file.

Meshopt decoding is bundled from Three.js. No external decoder CDN, account, or runtime model service is required.
