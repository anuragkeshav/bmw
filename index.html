What is this collection?

The Art of Motion is an automotive portfolio exploring BMW through editorial design, interactive 3D and cinematic motion. A collection to experience and admire — not a showroom or store.

Start exploring ↗
What makes an M car different?

BMW M brings motorsport thinking to the everyday. Purpose-built engines, carefully tuned suspension, distinctive aerodynamics and driver-focused details work together to create a more immediate connection with the road.

What can I interact with?

Rotate the M4 CSL, explore polished metallic finishes, and tap the bonnet itself to open or close it. Replay the cinematic entrance and enjoy the lighting and smoke. Every interaction is part of the visual study; finishes are artistic interpretations.

How do I explore the car in 3D?

Drag the car in the opening scene to change your perspective, or focus the scene and use your arrow keys. Select a colour swatch to explore a different metallic finish. Tap or click directly on the bonnet to reveal the engine, then tap it again to close. With the car focused, Enter or Space does the same, and P pauses or resumes the lighting and smoke. Replay opening restarts the entrance; the reset arrow returns to the closed-bonnet view. Reduced-motion mode shows the engine immediately and disables smoke.

Is this an official BMW website?

No. The Art of Motion is an independent design study celebrating BMW. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by BMW AG. Vehicle images and the interactive model are illustrative. External model links are included as references to the original BMW designs.
